# The Awakened Duelists (Playable Champions)

In *Vibrant Castle* you command two distinct duelists forged in the melancholic, warm aesthetic of gothic fantasy. Play either one solo, or both in shared-keyboard co-op. Both share the same movement kit (jump, melee strike, slide or dash, two spells, potions); their fighting style and look differ. See *Controls* for the keys.

---

## 1. Valen — Chrono Duelist

> *"There are no invincible foes. Only moments that have yet to be calculated."*

- **Role**: primary protagonist (solo mode, player 1 in co-op)
- **Archetype**: scholar, Bastion survivor, precision fencer
- **Combat philosophy**: economy of motion, precise rapier thrusts, temporal pacing

### Appearance

A long graphite travel greatcoat lined with golden thread, a practical waistcoat and boots built for sudden pivots. Rectangular spectacles and unruly chestnut hair mark a contemplative nature.

### Armament & Abilities

- **Bastion Steel Rapier**: a slender dueling blade made for quick thrusts into the weak points of clockwork constructs.
- **Solar Glyph**: concentrated solar energy that sears enemies.
- **Slide**: a low ground slide under projectile arcs and spinning blades. Forward is a slide, the opposite direction a backstep. It also reaches the dash-only secrets of the maps.

---

## 2. Aurelia — Aetherblade Warden

> *"Time may freeze in stone, but the light of Aether will always pierce the fracture."*

- **Role**: Order Warden (player 2 in co-op)
- **Archetype**: light warrior, ethereal fencer, agile guardian
- **Combat philosophy**: fluid acrobatics, wide sweeping cleaves, celestial beams

### Appearance

An athletic silhouette in deep cyan and polished brass, with an astral halo that trails light particles during fast movement.

### Armament & Abilities

- **Manifested Aetherblade**: a weapon shaped from celestial energy, built for wide cleaves through groups of enemies.
- **Phase Dash**: a quick spatial shift for dodging and for reaching dash-only secrets.
- **Celestial Beam**: a focused ray of astral light.
