# The Awakened Duelists (Playable Champions)

In *Vibrant Castle*, players take command of two distinct duelists forged in the melancholic, warm aesthetic of 19th-century gothic fantasy.

---

## 1. Valen — Chrono Duelist

> *"There are no invincible foes. Only moments that have yet to be calculated."*

- **Role**: Primary Protagonist (Solo Mode / Player 1 in Co-op)
- **Archetype**: Scholar · Bastion Survivor · Precision Fencer
- **Combat Philosophy**: Economy of motion, precision rapier thrusts, temporal pacing

### Appearance & Silhouette of Valen

Valen wears a long graphite travel greatcoat lined with subtle golden thread, a practical waistcoat, and fitted boots built for sudden pivots. His signature rectangular spectacles and unruly chestnut hair reflect his contemplative nature.

### Armament & Abilities of Valen

- **Bastion Steel Rapier**: A slender dueling weapon forged from tempered Bastion steel, engineered for instantaneous thrusts into the weak points of clockwork constructs.
- **Solar Glyph**: A burst of concentrated solar energy that sears enemies and reduces poise thresholds.
- **Chrono Slide**: Low-friction ground slide underneath projectile arcs and spinning blades.

---

## 2. Aurelia — Aetherblade Warden

> *"Time may freeze in stone, but the light of Aether will always pierce the fracture."*

- **Role**: Order Warden (Player 2 in Co-op Mode)
- **Archetype**: Light Warrior · Ethereal Fencer · Agile Guardian
- **Combat Philosophy**: Fluid acrobatics, wide sweeping cleaves, celestial beams

### Appearance & Silhouette of Aurelia

Aurelia features an aerodynamic, athletic silhouette accented in deep cyan and polished brass. An ethereal astral halo surrounds her form, trailing light particles during rapid movement and dodges.

### Armament & Abilities of Aurelia

- **Manifested Aetherblade**: A weapon shaped directly from celestial energy, capable of sweeping wide cleave arcs through clusters of enemies.
- **Phase Dash**: Intangible spatial shift allowing her to glide directly through incoming projectiles and monsters.
- **Celestial Beam**: A focused ray of pure astral light that penetrates heavy armor.
