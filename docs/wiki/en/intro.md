# The Citadel of Aether — Chronicle of Awakening

Welcome to **Vibrant Castle**, a 2D action-adventure platformer set in a melancholic gothic fantasy world bathed in the glow of an eternal golden autumn. It runs right in your browser, on keyboard or gamepad.

---

## 1. Design Direction

This is where the project is heading. Some parts are already playable, others are still planned.

- **A metroidvania** with the movement feel of *Mario*: responsive, precise and readable.
- **The look and mood of *Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow***: the style of maps, heroes and enemies, and a gloomy, elegant castle atmosphere.
- **An autumn palette**: amber light, deep stone, warm leaves and mist.
- **Bastion-style reactive narration**: an omniscient Narrator who comments on what you do.
- **Simplified Castlevania gameplay** that feels right on keyboard or gamepad. The pad uses only 2 triggers, 4 face buttons, and the bumpers as selectors for actions.
- **Two duelists**: Valen and Aurelia, solo or in shared-keyboard co-op.
- **A two-level menu**: one level for the game, one for development tools.

---

## 2. World & Narrative Background

At the heart of an ancient realm stands the **Citadel of Aether**, once the grand seat of astronomers, horological masters and fencing scholars. After a catastrophic fracture of the chronometric core, the whole fortress froze in **temporal stasis**. Clock hands stopped mid-revolution, brass gears locked amid the clouds, and golden leaves hung motionless over crumbling courtyards.

After centuries, two **Awakened Duelists** break free:

- **Valen**, the *Chrono Duelist*: an analytical scholar and Bastion survivor with a precision rapier and solar glyph magic.
- **Aurelia**, the *Aetherblade Warden*: an agile guardian with a blade of pure Aether.

Together they cross the Citadel, reactivate dormant levers, defeat corrupted clockwork guardians and restore the flow of time.

## 3. What You Can Play Today

- **Five large locations** linked by portals, from the sunlit Courtyard to the Grand Bastion. See *World & Maps*.
- **19 enemy templates** placed across the maps. See *Bestiary*. Enemies patrol and can be defeated; they do not damage the player yet.
- **Every map can be finished without the dash.** The dash opens secrets.
- **Three graphics tiers** that also change enemy art. See *Graphics Quality*.
- **Deterministic Canvas 2D engine**: fixed-step simulation with a passive React 19 overlay on top.
