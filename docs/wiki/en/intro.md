# Vibrant Castle — The 2034 Zone

> *"Autumn leaves on French stone, and a reception committee made of old museum plate. Still—there are worse Tuesdays in 2034."*

**Vibrant Castle** is a 2D autumn metroidvania set in our own world in the year **2034**, after a localized techno-magical cataclysm. It runs directly in your browser on keyboard or gamepad and is developed in the open on GitHub.

---

## 1. Design direction

- **Mario-like structure and movement**: planned as eight worlds with eight maps each. Jumping is crisp and forgiving, with variable jump height, coyote time, and jump buffering.
- **Elements of *Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow***: gothic spaces, heroes, varied enemies, absorbable souls, a bestiary, and secret routes that reward re-exploration.
- **Autumn mood and palette**: warm lantern amber, dark timber, drifting leaves, and weathered brass across the game and UI.
- ***Bastion*-style narration**: an in-world Narrator watches what you do and reacts with dry, classy understatement—keeping humour out of dramatic turning points.
- **Accessible combat**: melee strikes, focused spells, slides, and backsteps tuned for both keyboard and a restricted gamepad layout.
- **Two equal leads**: Valen and Aurelia, playable solo or side by side in local 2-player co-op.
- **A two-level menu**: a clean game dialog for players, and a separate developer dialog ("Tworzenie") compiled into development builds only.

---

## 2. The story so far

The year is **2034**. Civilisation did not collapse: daily life continues across a patchwork of open regions, censored feeds, and quarantined perimeters. Yet small pockets struck by a sudden **cataclysm** turned into something new—places where magic became real and linear time broke down. Nobody knows *exactly* why it happened; the closest explanation is a butterfly-effect collision between an unprepared ritual performed in a magic-less world and a distant high-energy physics experiment. A rift opened, binding laboratory and ritual site across space. Outside the cordon, states, researchers, religions, criminal syndicates, and emerging mages race to turn magic into a science.

*Vibrant Castle* takes place inside one such transformed **Zone**, sealed behind a defended **buffer zone** (who staffs the perimeter remains a mystery). Within it, a **summoned demon and its hosts** reshaped real 2034 sites—a historic French château, a quarry ravine, underground heating tunnels, a robotics factory, a synthesis lab, an elevated aqueduct, and a fortified perimeter—reinterpreting 21st-century engineering in feudal and occult terms.

Two longtime friends, **Valen** (*Sun Knight*) and **Aurelia** (*Arcane Scholar*), find themselves pushed together by Zone reality. Neither is the sole protagonist; their partnership moves like Brazilian zouk, where proposals and responses pass back and forth as priorities shift. Watching them from somewhere within the world is a composed, deadpan **Narrator** whose identity is revealed only later.

## 3. What you can play today

- **Eight worlds, each with its first map (map 1) playable.** Portals connect all eight in a continuous circuit. See *World & Maps*.
- **Nineteen enemy templates**—sixteen fighting foes and three stationary training targets. Enemies patrol, hover, and can be defeated; **they do not damage the player yet** (enemy attacks arrive in phase 5). See *Bestiary*.
- **Keyboard and gamepad controls** with full remapping and a restricted pad layout. See *Controls*.
- **Three graphics tiers**: `hi8bit`, `hi16bit`, and `high`. See *Graphics*.
- **A deterministic Canvas 2D engine** paired with a passive React 19 overlay. See *Architecture*.

## 4. What is planned

The full campaign targets **8 worlds × 8 maps (64 stages)**. Maps 2–8 of every world, active enemy attacks, the six-attribute system, ability-gated backtracking, a map screen, and reactive dialogue scenes arrive in upcoming phases. See *What's New* for the phase roadmap.
