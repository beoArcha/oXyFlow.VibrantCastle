# The Citadel of Aether: Chronicle of Awakening

> *"The clocks froze in the golden hour of autumn, yet the stonework remembers every step of those who dare to break the silence."*

**Vibrant Castle** is a 2D gothic metroidvania in an eternal golden autumn. It runs in your browser, on keyboard or gamepad, and it is built in the open on GitHub.

---

## 1. Design direction

- **Mario-like structure and movement**: eight worlds with eight maps each. Movement is responsive and forgiving: variable jump, coyote time and jump buffering.
- **The mood of *Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow***: map style, heroes, enemies, souls and a bestiary, with worlds that reward re-exploration.
- **An autumn palette**: amber light, dark timber, drifting leaves, in the game and in the UI.
- **Bastion-style narration**: a Narrator who comments on what you do.
- **Simple combat for keyboard or pad**: melee, a few spells and a dash.
- **Two duelists**: Valen and Aurelia, solo or in local 2-player co-op.
- **A two-level menu**: the game dialog for play, and a separate developer dialog that exists only in development builds.

---

## 2. The story so far

At the heart of an ancient realm stands the **Citadel of Aether**, once the seat of horologists, astronomers and fencing scholars. After a fracture of the chronometric core, the whole fortress froze in **temporal stasis**. Clock hands stopped mid-turn, brass gears locked in the clouds, and golden leaves hang still over the courtyards.

Two **Awakened Duelists** break free: **Valen**, the Solar Knight, and **Aurelia**, the Scholar of Arcanum. Together they cross the eight linked worlds of the Citadel and restore the flow of time.

---

## 3. What you can play today

- **Eight worlds, each with its first map (map 1) playable.** The worlds form a loop through portals. See *World & Maps*.
- **Nineteen enemy templates**, of which sixteen fight and three are training targets. Enemies patrol, hover and guard their ground, and they can be defeated. **They do not damage the player yet**; enemy attacks are planned. See *Bestiary*.
- **Keyboard and pad controls** with a restricted pad layout. See *Controls*.
- **Three graphics tiers**: hi-8bit, hi-16bit and high. See *Graphics*.
- **A deterministic Canvas 2D engine** with a passive React overlay. See *Architecture*.

## 4. What is planned

The campaign is planned as **8 worlds x 8 maps (64 maps)**. Maps 2 to 8 of every world, enemy attacks, ability gating and a map screen come in later phases. The roadmap in *What's New* lists the phases.
