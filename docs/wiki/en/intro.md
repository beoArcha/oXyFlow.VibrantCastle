# The Citadel of Aether — Chronicle of Awakening

Welcome to **Vibrant Castle**, a 2D action-adventure platformer set in a melancholic gothic fantasy world bathed in the glow of an eternal golden autumn.

---

## 1. World & Narrative Background

At the heart of an ancient realm stands the **Citadel of Aether** — once the grand seat of astronomers, horological masters, and fencing scholars. Following a catastrophic fracture of the chronometric core, the entire fortress became suspended in **temporal stasis**. The hands of monumental clocktowers froze mid-revolution, brass gears locked amidst the clouds, and golden leaves hung motionless above crumbling stone courtyards.

After centuries of stasis, two **Awakened Duelists** break free from slumber:

- **Valen**, the *Chrono Duelist* — an analytical scholar and Bastion survivor wielding a precision steel rapier and solar glyph magic.
- **Aurelia**, the *Aetherblade Warden* — an agile guardian commanding celestial energy and a manifested blade of pure Aether.

Together, they must journey through the interconnected sectors of the Citadel, reactivate dormant chronometric levers, vanquish corrupted clockwork guardians, and restore the flow of time under the watchful eye of an omniscient **Narrator**.

---

## 2. Visual Direction & Gameplay Philosophy

*Vibrant Castle* marries three distinct artistic traditions:

- **Atmospheric Warmth of Bastion & Hades**: A rich palette of deep stone, dark wood, and vibrant amber lighting with dynamic reactive narration.
- **Pixel Art Clarity of Owlboy**: Clean geometric silhouettes, strict depth planes, and five distinct parallax tiers.
- **Kinetic Combat of Castlevania**: Lightning-fast thrusts, sweeping aether arcs, low-profile sliding under obstacles, and directional phase dashes (`Chrono Dash`).

---

## 3. Key Features

- **Co-op & Solo Modes**: Experience the journey solo or together in two-player shared-keyboard cooperative play.
- **Interconnected Citadel Sectors**: From the sunlit Courtyard of Awakening to the harrowing Chasm of Thorns and the vertical heights of the Clockwork Tower.
- **Deterministic Canvas 2D Engine**: 60 Hz fixed-update physics with a decoupled passive React 19 overlay ensuring zero-latency control responsiveness.
