# World & Maps: the Five Locations

The Citadel of Aether is made of **five large locations** linked by portals. All five were rebuilt much larger than before. Sizes below are in pixels (width × height).

**Portal route**: Courtyard of Awakening → Chasm of Thorns → Clockwork Tower → Aether Sanctuary → Grand Bastion → back to the Courtyard (the victory loop). Each portal also works in reverse, except that the Grand Bastion's last portal closes the loop.

**Every map can be finished without the dash.** The dash (slide) opens secrets: hidden lofts, ledges and isles with extra rewards.

**Gates** lift with a 1.2 s portcullis animation when their lever opens them, and their collision shrinks with them. **Decor** (lanterns, braziers, banners, crystals and more) is listed for each location below. It is purely visual and never blocks you.

---

## 1. Courtyard of Awakening — 7200 × 1350

A sunlit flagstone yard in golden-ochre twilight, with falling oak leaves. Your journey begins here.

- **Zones, west to east**: Awakening Yard (safe first steps), Arcade Terraces, Fountain Basin, Gatehouse, Chasm Road.
- **Mechanism**: a golden lever on the parapet raises the iron portcullis.
- **Secrets**: a loft above the arcade and a ledge in the fountain basin.
- **Decor**: garden lamp posts, hanging lanterns, wall torches, tattered banners and leaf piles.
- **Enemies**: Straw Training Dummy, Courtyard Sentinel, Gear Mite, Gargoyle Scout, Lantern Warden.

## 2. Chasm of Thorns and Shadows — 6000 × 1500

A crimson-amber gorge of crumbling foundations, with a floor of amber crystal spikes.

- **Zones**: Entry Floor & Descent, Upper & Low Ledges (with a low arch to slide under), Isles & Bridges, Lever & Portcullis, Climb with a moving platform, Exit.
- **Secrets**: a hidden step and alcove, plus three dash-only routes.
- **Decor**: ember skull lanterns, thorn brambles, iron chains and wall torches.
- **Enemies**: Thorn Creeper, Chasm Gargoyle, Chasm Crawler, Gear Mite.

## 3. Clockwork Tower — 3600 × 3000 (vertical)

The inside of the Great Horologium: brass gear trains, pendulums and a motorised lift. This is the tall map; you climb five storeys.

- **Zones, bottom to top**: Foundry floor, Gear gallery, Pendulum shaft, Escapement hall, Belfry.
- **Mechanism**: strike the Gear lever to open the spire gate.
- **Secrets**: a secret chain in the pendulum shaft.
- **Decor**: hanging lanterns, iron chains and wall gears.
- **Enemies**: Straw Training Dummy, Gear Mite, Clockwork Automaton, Lantern Warden, Gargoyle Scout.

## 4. Aether Sanctuary — 6400 × 1600

Floating islets and ruined arcades above the clouds, lit in cyan and indigo.

- **Zones**: Arrival Terrace, Shard Field, Cloister & Altar, Upper Steps & Parapet, Secrets & Dash Isle.
- **Mechanism**: the altar lever opens the gateway to the Grand Bastion.
- **Secrets**: three hidden ledges and an isle reachable only by dash.
- **Decor**: aether crystal clusters and candelabra.
- **Enemies**: Aether Sentinel, Aether Wisp, Chrono Hound, Lantern Warden and the elite Aether Archon at the gateway.

## 5. Grand Bastion — 8000 × 1500

The longest map, a royal-gold circuit that echoes all earlier places. Finishing it loops back to the Courtyard.

- **Zones**: Barbican, Rampart, Case & Cross Ledges (with dash rails), Keep & Treasury, Ward & Arena.
- **Mechanism**: two sluice levers open the keep gate and the ward gate.
- **Secrets**: a hidden step among the cross ledges, and the dash rails.
- **Decor**: war braziers, wall torches, tattered banners, a hanging lantern and iron chains.
- **Enemies**: Straw Training Dummy, Courtyard Sentinel (and a boss variant in the arena), Clockwork Automaton, Chasm Gargoyle, Chrono Hound, Gear Mite, Lantern Warden.
