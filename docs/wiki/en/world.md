# World & Maps: The Zone & Eight Worlds

After the **2028 cataclysm**, civilisation kept moving into **2034**—except along the belt where magic (**Aether**) took root and time lost its straight edge. Magic is only a few years old and works worldwide, though it weakens with distance; it is strongest inside the **Zone** (at the laboratory and the cult site) and stronger at known mystical, natural, and shamanic places around the world.

Surrounding the Zone lies a **state-defended buffer zone** stretching from **Utah to Switzerland**—directly affected over a dozen or so kilometers, with emanations across tens of kilometers and twice as wide in places. Flying over at altitude is possible, while driving through or flying low is risky; **France and the USA** are building underground tunnels to reconnect. At sea, water dispersed most of the effect into thread-like emanations where strange creatures appear, though it remains our world. Even so, people come seeking the philosopher's stone, wealth, a panacea, or adventure. Inside the Zone, a **summoned demon and its hosts** mistook 21st-century industry and science for medieval craft, warping a historic French château and neighboring modern facilities into eight linked gothic stages.

The game features **eight worlds** connected by portals in a loop. The full campaign plan is **8 worlds × 8 maps (64 stages)**. Today, **each world has its first map (map 1)** built and playable; maps 2–8 are planned for future phases.

## The portal loop

Courtyard of Awakening → Chasm of Thorns and Shadows → Ashen Catacombs → Clockwork Tower & Angled Range → Alchemist's Library → Aether Sky Sanctuary & Aqueduct → Sunlit Bastion → Eclipse Crown → back to the Courtyard.

*(At the summit of the Eclipse Crown, broken time folds back onto the château threshold, creating a cyclic loop ahead of the full campaign finale.)*

## Map design

- Every map offers a clear primary flow with zones, levers, and portcullises. Striking a lever visibly raises its linked gate over `1.2 s`.
- Every main route can be completed without dashing; dashes and low slides unlock optional ledges and secret alcoves.
- Scenery props—lanterns, torches, braziers, and crystals—cast warm autumn light and never obstruct movement.
