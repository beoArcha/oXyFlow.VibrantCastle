# World & Maps: the Eight Worlds

The Citadel of Aether has **eight worlds**, linked by portals in a loop. The campaign plan is **8 worlds x 8 maps**, which makes 64 maps in total. Today **each world has its first map (map 1)** built and playable. Maps 2 to 8 are planned.

## The portal loop

Courtyard of Awakening → Chasm of Thorns and Shadows → Ashen Catacombs → Clockwork Tower → Alchemist's Library → Aether Sky Sanctuary → Grand Bastion → Eclipse Crown → back to the Courtyard.

## The worlds

| # | World (map 1) | Atmosphere and role |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Courtyard of Awakening** | Golden autumn courtyard with falling leaves, where the journey starts. A lever raises the portcullis. |
| 2 | **Chasm of Thorns and Shadows** | An overgrown gorge with crystal spikes, briar hazards and moving platforms. |
| 3 | **Ashen Catacombs** | Smouldering crypts beneath the citadel's foundations. |
| 4 | **Clockwork Tower & Angled Range** | The inside of the Great Horologium: brass gears, pendulums and a tall vertical climb. |
| 5 | **Alchemist's Library** | A labyrinth of shelves, alembics and forbidden tomes. |
| 6 | **Aether Sky Sanctuary & Aqueduct** | Floating islets and arcades above the clouds, lit in cyan and indigo. |
| 7 | **Grand Bastion Training Grounds** | A royal-gold fortress circuit with heavy ramparts and a keep. |
| 8 | **Eclipse Crown** | The summit under a twin eclipse, the climax of the campaign. |

## Map design

- Every map has a readable flow, zones, levers and gates. Gates lift with a 1.2 s portcullis animation.
- Each map can be finished without the dash. The dash (slide) reaches secrets: hidden ledges, lofts and isles.
- Decor such as lanterns, braziers, banners and crystals is purely visual and never blocks the player.
