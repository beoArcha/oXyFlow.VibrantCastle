# Graphics Quality

The graphics quality setting (in the in-game menu, display settings) changes how heroes, enemies and the world are drawn. Enemy art follows the setting too.

| Setting | Enemy art | What it looks like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **hi-8bit** | Chunky sprites in the style of 8-bit games | Flat colours from a small palette, hard outlines and blocky shapes, kept sharp on today's screens. The lightest option for weaker devices. |
| **hi-16bit** | Richer sprites in the style of 16-bit games | More colours, simple shading and highlights, still crisp pixel edges. |
| **high** | Painted animation sheets | Full-colour painted frames with the most detail. |

Enemies without a painted sheet use the hi-16bit art on **high**, so no enemy is ever left without a sprite.

Changes apply immediately, with no restart: heroes and enemies switch to the chosen tier as soon as you pick it.

Both pixel-art styles are small vector files, so they load quickly and stay sharp on any screen.

If the game feels slow on your device, lower the quality first; the level layout and gameplay stay exactly the same on every setting.
