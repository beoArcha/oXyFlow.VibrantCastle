# Graphics Quality

The graphics quality setting (in the in-game menu, display settings) changes how heroes, enemies and the world are drawn. Enemy art now follows the setting too.

| Quality | Enemy art | Style |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low** | **NES class** sprites | Very simple, few colours, chunky shapes. Lightest on weak devices. |
| **Medium** | **SNES class** sprites | Richer colour and shading. These are tiny vector files, so they stay small and sharp. |
| **High** | Painted animation sheets | Hand-painted frames with full animation. |

Enemies that do not have a painted sheet yet use the SNES class art on High, so no enemy is ever left without a sprite.

Changes apply immediately, with no restart: heroes and enemies switch to the chosen tier as soon as you pick it.

If the game feels slow on your device, lower the quality first; the level layout and gameplay stay exactly the same on every tier.
