# Bestiary

The game has **19 enemy templates**. Sixteen are fighting creatures and three are training targets that never fight back.

**Current status:** enemies patrol, hover or guard their ground, and they can be defeated. They **do not damage the player yet**. Enemy attacks and contact damage are planned for phase 5.

Health values below are the template values from the game data.

---

## Training targets

- **Training Dummy** (60 HP): a straw-and-oak target.
- **Training Target** (45 HP): a shooting target on the terraces and in the clocktower.
- **Grand Training Dummy** (120 HP): a larger dummy for the Courtyard.

## Fighting creatures

- **Gear Mite** (35 HP): a tiny brass skitterer that runs along the floor. Found in the Courtyard, Chasm, Catacombs and Clocktower.
- **Courtyard Sentinel** (80 HP): a bronze guard that patrols the flagstones. Found in the Courtyard and on the Grand Bastion circuit.
- **Clockwork Owl Gargoyle** (75 HP): a clockwork owl that hunts over the Courtyard, Clocktower and Sanctuary.
- **Gargoyle Scout** (55 HP): a light brass flyer on a high patrol line.
- **Chasm Gargoyle** (70 HP): an iron flyer above the gorge.
- **Chasm Crawler** (55 HP): a low, scuttling beast under the slabs.
- **Thorn Creeper** (65 HP): rooted thorny flora that holds one spot.
- **Aether Wisp** (30 HP): a restless orb of starlight, found in several worlds.
- **Lantern Warden** (70 HP): a slow floating sentry with a lantern.
- **Chrono Hound** (85 HP): a fast clockwork beast on ground patrol.
- **Clockwork Automaton** (130 HP): a heavy brass soldier, slow and sturdy.
- **Aether Sentinel** (110 HP): a crystalline guardian on aether ribbons.
- **Shadow Knight** (160 HP): an elite phantom duelist, placed in the Library, the Catacombs, the Grand Bastion and the Eclipse Crown.
- **Bastion Gargoyle** (105 HP): a heavy gargoyle of the Bastion.

## Bosses and elites

- **Aether Archon** (220 HP): the elite guardian of the Sanctuary gateway; it also appears in the Eclipse Crown.
- **Bastion Sentinel Commander** (280 HP): the commander of the Grand Bastion arena; it also appears in the Eclipse Crown.
