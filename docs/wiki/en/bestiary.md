# Bestiary

The game has **19 enemy templates**. Fourteen are distinct creatures; the other five are variants that reuse the art and role of one of them (noted below). Four creatures are new: **Gear Mite**, **Thorn Creeper**, **Lantern Warden** and **Chrono Hound**.

**Current status**: enemies patrol, hover or stand guard, and they can be defeated for score and skill points. They do **not damage the player yet**; attacks are planned.

---

## Where Each Enemy Lives

### Courtyard of Awakening

- **Straw Training Dummy** (60 HP): a straw-and-oak target that never fights back. Good for learning your reach.
- **Courtyard Sentinel** (80 HP): a bronze guard patrolling the flagstones.
- **Gear Mite** (20 HP, new): a tiny brass skitterer that scurries along the floor.
- **Gargoyle Scout** (50 HP): a light brass flyer on a high patrol line.
- **Lantern Warden** (35 HP, new): a slow floating sentry with a lantern.

### Chasm of Thorns

- **Thorn Creeper** (50 HP, new): rooted thorny flora that holds one spot.
- **Chasm Gargoyle** (65 HP): an iron flyer lurking above the gorge.
- **Chasm Crawler** (80 HP): a low, scorpion-like beast under the slabs.
- **Gear Mite** (new): also scurries here.

### Clockwork Tower

- **Straw Training Dummy**, **Gear Mite**, **Gargoyle Scout** and **Lantern Warden** appear again on the lower storeys.
- **Clockwork Automaton** (120 HP): a heavy brass soldier, slow and sturdy.

### Aether Sanctuary

- **Aether Sentinel** (110 HP): a crystalline guardian floating on aether ribbons.
- **Aether Wisp** (60 HP): a restless orb of starlight, three of them here.
- **Chrono Hound** (60 HP, new): a fast clockwork beast on ground patrol.
- **Lantern Warden** (new): floats through the arcades.
- **Aether Archon** (220 HP): the elite miniboss at the gateway end.

### Grand Bastion

- **Straw Training Dummy** (plus the variants **grand_dummy** and **training_target**): training targets on the circuit.
- **Courtyard Sentinel**, with the boss variant **Bastion Sentinel** (100 HP, elite) in the Ward & Arena.
- **Clockwork Automaton**, **Chrono Hound**, **Gear Mite** and **Lantern Warden**.
- **Chasm Gargoyle**, including two **Bastion Gargoyle** variants (50 HP).

---

## Weaknesses

Every enemy has elemental resistances; a negative value is a weakness. Fire is the most common weakness (the straw dummies, thorn creepers, crawlers, gargoyles and mites), frost bites the heavy constructs (Automaton, Sentinel), and the sanctuary guardians (Aether Sentinel, Wisp and Archon) resist astral magic hard but fear shadow. The Lantern Warden resists fire.

- **Clockwork Owl Gargoyle** (45 HP): a lighter owl form of the Gargoyle Scout; no map places it yet.
