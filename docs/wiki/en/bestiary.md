# Bestiary

The game has **19 enemy templates**. Sixteen are fighting creatures and three are training targets that never fight back.

**Current status:** enemies patrol, hover or guard their ground, and they can be defeated. They **do not damage the player yet**. Enemy attacks and contact damage are planned for phase 5.

Names are the ones shown in the game, Polish first, then English. Health values are the template values from the game data.

---

## Training targets

- **Kukła Treningowa** / *Training Dummy* (60 HP): a straw-and-oak dummy in the Courtyard.
- **Kukła Treningowa** / *Training Dummy* (45 HP): a shooting target on the terraces of the Clockwork Tower and the Courtyard.
- **Kukła Treningowa** / *Training Dummy* (120 HP): a larger dummy in the Courtyard.

## Fighting creatures

- **Trybik** / *Gear Mite* (35 HP): a tiny brass skitterer that runs along the floor.
- **Strażnik Dziedzińca** / *Courtyard Sentinel* (80 HP): a bronze guard that patrols the flagstones.
- **Gargulec z Rozpadliny** / *Chasm Gargoyle* (75 HP): a clockwork owl that hunts over the Courtyard, the Clockwork Tower and the Sanctuary.
- **Zwiadowca Gargulców** / *Gargoyle Scout* (55 HP): a light brass flyer on a high patrol line.
- **Gargulec z Rozpadliny** / *Chasm Gargoyle* (70 HP): an iron flyer above the gorge.
- **Pnącze Otchłani** / *Chasm Crawler* (55 HP): a low, scuttling beast under the slabs.
- **Cierniowy Pędnik** / *Thorn Creeper* (65 HP): rooted thorny flora that holds one spot.
- **Iskra Aetheru** / *Aether Wisp* (30 HP): a restless orb of starlight, found in several worlds.
- **Latarniowy Stróż** / *Lantern Warden* (70 HP): a slow floating sentry with a lantern.
- **Chrono-Ogar** / *Chrono Hound* (85 HP): a fast clockwork beast on ground patrol.
- **Zegarowy Automat** / *Clockwork Automaton* (130 HP): a heavy brass soldier, slow and sturdy.
- **Strażnik Aetheru** / *Aether Sentinel* (110 HP): a crystalline guardian on aether ribbons.
- **Cień Rycerza** / *Shadow Knight* (160 HP): an elite phantom duelist, placed in the Library, the Catacombs, the Grand Bastion and the Eclipse Crown.
- **Gargulec z Rozpadliny** / *Chasm Gargoyle* (105 HP): a heavy gargoyle of the Grand Bastion.

## Bosses and elites

- **Archont Aetheru** / *Aether Archon* (220 HP): the elite guardian of the Sanctuary gateway; it also appears in the Eclipse Crown.
- **Strażnik Dziedzińca** / *Courtyard Sentinel* (280 HP): the commander of the Grand Bastion arena, which the game lists under the Courtyard Sentinel name; it also appears in the Eclipse Crown.

### Shared names

Some templates share a display name in the current build, so the game shows the same label for them. The three training dummies all read *Training Dummy*, the clockwork owl and the Grand Bastion gargoyle read *Chasm Gargoyle*, and the Bastion commander reads *Courtyard Sentinel*. The entries above describe each one by its HP and place.
