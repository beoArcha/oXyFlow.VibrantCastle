# Playable Heroes & Champions (Characters)

In *Vibrant Castle*, players command two distinct duelists forged in the melancholic, warm aesthetic of 19th-century gothic fantasy. Each character possesses unique kinetics, bespoke animation tracks, distinct spell constellations, and divergent philosophies regarding combat and the flow of Aether.

---

## 1. Valen – Chrono Duelist / Bastion Survivor

* **Primary Role**: Main Protagonist (Solo Mode / Player 1)
* **Archetype**: Scholar · Explorer · Precision Duelist
* **Combat Philosophy**: Control · Economy of Motion · Analytical Execution

---

### Identity & Background

Valen is not a theatrical warrior seeking glory — he is a scholar and survivor shaped by ancient ruins and temporal anomalies. Having awakened upon the sunlit ramparts of the Grand Bastion, Valen approaches the castle not with reckless zeal, but with surgical calculation.

Every movement has purpose. Every action is the product of deliberate calculation rather than instinct. It is not raw muscle that defines Valen, but absolute spatial mastery and emotional composure.

---

### Silhouette, Appearance & Attire

* **Greatcoat**: Long graphite/charcoal travel coat tailored with subtle gilded embroidery and reinforced hems suited for navigating crumbling masonry.
* **Layers**: Practical scholar’s waistcoat, fitted trousers, and rugged leather boots engineered for swift pivots and balanced footwork.
* **Hair**: Short, slightly unruly chestnut hair ruffled by autumn breezes.
* **Eyewear**: Signature rectangular-rimmed spectacles — a defining element reflecting his analytical perception of reality.
* **Expression**: Calm, watchful, stoic; subtle and reserved facial demeanor.

**Color Palette**:

* Graphite / Deep Slate Charcoal (`#1e293b`, `#334155`)
* Muted Leather Brown / Ochre (`#78350f`, `#92400e`)
* Warm Radiant Gold / Amber Accents (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`)

---

### Movement Mechanics & Kinetic Style

* **Minimalist & Efficient**: Zero wasted flourishes or unnecessary theatrical windups.
* **Low Center of Gravity**: Balanced stance prioritizing instant recovery after attacks, slides, and backsteps.
* **Natural Cloth Dynamics**: Realistic settling of coat-tails during sprints, vertical jumps, and sudden stops.
* *Valen does not lash out — he executes clean, geometric transitions through space.*

---

### Armament

#### Precision Steel Rapier

* A slender, balanced dueling rapier forged from tempered Bastion steel.
* Optimized for lightning-fast thrusts, crisp horizontal slashes, and defensive deflections.
* Slices clean lines through autumn air with audible whistling resonance.

---

### Signature Abilities & Disciplines

1. **Precision Slash (Standard Attack)**:
   * A swift, controlled arc of the rapier. Minimal blade travel, maximum kinetic transfer.
2. **Chrono Dash (Evasion)**:
   * A linear displacement through space accompanied by subtle temporal afterimages. Rather than an explosive burst, it represents a calculated shift along the time axis.
3. **Glyph Casting (Magic Inscription)**:
   * Manifests ordered celestial energy in the form of geometric, solar glyphs. Predictable, razor-sharp, and devoid of chaotic distortion.

---

### Magic Discipline: Solar & Fortitude Arts

Valen channels the radiant, ordered energy of the Sun and Bastion architecture:

* **Solar Flare** (`solar_flare`): Starter instantaneous beam of concentrated solar brilliance piercing castle gloom.
* **Solar Shield Bash** (`shield_bash`): Defensive barrier deflection repelling close-quarters hostiles.
* **Solar Fireball** (`solar_fireball`): Explosive sphere of radiant flame inflicting severe burst damage across packed groups.
* **Prismatic Barrier** (`prismatic_shield`): Shimmering golden aegis granting complete invulnerability to spikes and strikes for 3 seconds.
* **Radiant Slash** (`radiant_strike`): Master Tier 3 threefold shockwave of condensed sunbeams slicing across the arena.
* **Solar Sentinel** (`sun_sentinel`): Automatic flame familiar orbiting Valen, striking nearby threats every 2 seconds.
* **Blade Infusion** (`blade_infusion`): Passive enchantment infusing rapier slashes with permanent solar warmth (+15 Attack).
* **Boundless Vigor** (`stamina_surge`): Passive conditioning granting +25 Max Stamina and expedited dash recovery.
* **Champion's Fortitude** (`vitality_surge`): Bodily endurance granting +35 Max HP and +8 Armor.

---

### Design Principles (Sprite & Animation Asset Standards)

* **Clear Silhouette**: Distinct readability in low, mid, and high resolution atlas sheets.
* **Rooted Footwork**: Strict foot-level anchor point (pivot at normalized `(0.5, 1.0)`) across all animation frames (`idle`, `run`, `jump`, `fall`, `attack`, `dash`, `cast`, `crouch`).
* **Controlled FX**: Effects remain bounded within character sprite cells, emphasizing crisp geometric clarity over unbounded particle chaos.

## 2. Aurelia – Aetherblade Warden / Bastion Protector

* **Primary Role**: Companion Champion (Co-op Mode / Player 2)
* **Archetype**: Warrior · Warlock · Protector (Arcane Ranger-Sorceress)
* **Combat Philosophy**: Flow · Instinctive Reaction · Organic Area Dominance

---

### Background & Identity

Where Valen analyzes, Aurelia perceives. Where he plans, she acts.

Aurelia is a living conduit to the Aetherial currents pulsing through the castle foundations. Her power does not stem from geometric formulas, but from intuition, fluidity, and predatory reflex. While Valen stands as a calculated observer in the ruins, Aurelia moves through them as if native to their ancient, shifting halls.

---

### Silhouette, Attire & Appearance

* **Flowing Robes**: Layered silk and velvet robes in rich shades of imperial violet and twilight purple, adorned with ritualistic gold filigree.
* **Hair & Movement**: Long, undulating dark hair that reacts dynamically to movement, acceleration, and ambient Aether currents.
* **Aetherial Aura**: Subtle luminescent glow radiating from her gaze and fingertip conduits when mana surges.
* **Jewelry & Talismans**: Gilded hoops and resonant crystal pendants that sway in harmony with her acrobatic strides.
* **Expression**: Confident, alert, intuitive, slightly mysterious.

**Color Palette**:

* Deep Imperial Violet / Twilight Purple (`#4c1d95`, `#581c87`, `#7e22ce`)
* Radiant Cerulean / Aether Cyan (`#06b6d4`, `#22d3ee`)
* Ornate Ancient Gold (`#f59e0b`, `#d97706`)

---

### Kinetic Style & Movement Mechanics

* **Fluid & Predatory**: Inspired by feline locomotion, balance, and boundless agility.
* **High Kinetic Amplitude**: Seamless transitions between sprints, low crouches, and aerial somersaults without losing momentum.
* **Continuous Flow**: Each attack and evasion chains into the next movement pattern like a choreographed dance.

---

### Armaments

#### Manifested Aetherblade

* An ethereal blade condensed from pure atmospheric Aether.
* Can fluctuate between a solid cutting edge and a sweeping crescent arc of arcane plasma.
* Strikes with sweeping, pulling momentum rather than single-point thrusts, dominating wide corridors.

---

### Signature Disciplines & Abilities

1. **Aether Arc (Standard Attack)**:
   * A broad, crescent slash of humming arcane energy covering wide horizontal and diagonal arcs.
2. **Phase Dash (Evasion)**:
   * An agile phase-shift accompanied by spectral chromatic dispersion, slipping effortlessly through hostile hitboxes and hazard traps.
3. **Pulse Casting (Arcane Weaving)**:
   * Emitters of pulsing, living energy forms that ripple across the battlefield in organic waves.

---

### Magic Discipline: Celestial & Temporal Arts

Aurelia commands the primordial fabric of Aether, gravity, and temporal dilation:

* **Aether Bolt** (`aether_bolt`): Starter multi-directional beam of celestial force aimable straight, vertical (90°), or at 45° angles.
* **Aether Barrier** (`aether_barrier`): Protective celestial shroud absorbing kinetic shock and hazard spikes.
* **Chrono Frost Nova** (`frost_nova`): Radial explosion of 8 freezing ice needles halting enemy advances in all directions.
* **Temporal Phase / Chrono Rewind** (`chrono_rewind`): Bends the temporal continuum around Aurelia, granting extended invulnerability and heightened agility.
* **Celestial Comet** (`celestial_comet`): Master Tier 3 incantation summoning a devastating cosmic comet of raw Aether with wide area impact.
* **Sentinel Spark** (`sentinel_spark`): Autonomous celestial wisp discharging automatic electric bolts at nearby enemies every 2.5 seconds.
* **Mana Wellspring** (`mana_font`): Passive attunement granting +25 Max Mana and accelerating passive mana restoration.
* **Scholar's Focus** (`scholarly_focus`): Intellectual discipline elevating spell efficiency and increasing mana recovery rate by +25%.
* **Astral Harmony** (`astral_attunement`): Ultimate cosmic attunement yielding +30 Mana, +20 HP, and lightning-fast resource cycling.

### Asset & Animation Specifications (v4.0 Standard)

1. **Resolution Scalability**: Fully supported across `low` (64x64/57x71), `mid` (85x85/114x143), and `high` (128x128/114x143) variants.
2. **Animation Tracks**: Complete 8-track layout: `idle`, `run` (or `move`), `jump`, `fall`, `attack` (wide frames supported), `dash` (wide motion trail), `cast` (spell channel), and `crouch`.
3. **Portraits Matrix**: 5x5 grid (25 distinct emotional expressions and moods) utilized across dynamic dialogue prompts, HUD status cards, and the Character Studio.
