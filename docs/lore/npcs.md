# Non-Playable Characters, Allies & Fauna (NPCs & Guides)

The world of *Vibrant Castle* is brought to life through legendary mentors, mysterious animal companions, and the resonant voice of the citadel's chronicler, who witnesses and commemorates every step of the heroes' ascension.

---

## 1. The Bastion Narrator (The Voice of the Past / The Chronicler)

* **Role**: Omniscient Storyteller & Reactive Acoustic Guide (Inspired by *Bastion*)
* **Manifestation**:
  * Not physically bound to the arena floor; instead manifests via the dynamic **Bastion Narrator Bar** along the lower HUD overlay.
  * Speaks in a rich, warm, melancholic baritone that reacts dynamically to player agency, successes, close calls, and setbacks in real time.
* **Personality & Philosophy**:
  * Calibrated, observant, quietly nostalgic. He regards the duelists with quiet admiration rather than hollow cheers.
  * Treats the castle not merely as a tomb of stones, but as a living clockwork organism waiting to be wound once more.
* **Signature In-Game Commentary (from `narrations.ts`)**:
  * *On Awakening*:
    > *"The youth awoke upon the vast castle grounds. The crisp air carried scents of dry autumn leaves and fresh aether."*
  * *On Movement & Footwork*:
    > *"They took their first strides, discovering how low slides and agile backsteps grant total mastery of space."*
  * *On Blade Combat*:
    > *"He swung the blade. Tempered steel sliced through the autumn breeze with a sharp hiss."*
  * *On Striking Levers*:
    > *"The switch resisted, but a decisive slash or spell blast freed the frozen gears."*
  * *On Opening Castle Gates*:
    > *"Deep within the clocktower, massive iron cogs groaned, lifting the portcullis toward the clouds."*
  * *On Stepping into Portals*:
    > *"And so they stepped into a new dawn, fully armed and attuned to ancient Aether."*

## 2. Corvus – The Solar Raven (Animal Companion & Aerial Scout)

* **Role**: Celestial Scout & Aerial Sentinel
* **Appearance**:
  * An aristocratic raven with lustrous obsidian-and-gold plumage, iridescent violet highlights, and warm amber eyes.
  * Wears a tiny engraved brass ring around its left leg bearing the guild seal of the Grand Bastion.
* **Function in the Castle**:
  * Perches gracefully on pivotal ledge corners, high parapets, and moving crane arms, signaling safe landing spots across perilous chasms.
  * Takes flight with an urgent warning caw whenever flying gargoyles or descending clockwork owls initiate their dive runs.
  * Directs player attention toward hidden wall levers, secret treasure caches, and obscured archway shortcuts.
* **Key In-Game Dialogue (from `dialogues.ts`)**:
  > **Corvus (Raven)**: *"Kraa! The summit clock is ticking in reverse. Beware the rogue clockwork automatons!"*

---

## 3. Pip / Amber – The Chrono-Cat (Pip the Chrono-Cat / Bursztynek)

* **Role**: Save Crypt Guardian & Aetheric Resonator
* **Appearance**:
  * A plush, russet-furred ginger cat sporting a tailored dark leather collar fitted with a miniature, functioning brass hourglass.
  * Golden eyes that dilate with faint cerulean luminescence when in proximity to concentrated Aether currents.
* **Function in the Castle**:
  * Resides within the meta-game interface known as the **Save Crypt** (`save.ts`).
  * Oversees the secure serialization, export, and import of world states (`.json` chronicles), ensuring data integrity.
  * Purrs in harmonic frequency with Aether crystals, signaling when players possess sufficient mana or items to survive upcoming hazard gauntlets.
* **Key In-Game Dialogue (from `dialogues.ts`)**:
  > **Amber (Cat)**: *"Meow... The subterranean aether is surging. Use your prismatic shield to traverse the thorn bed safely."*

## 4. The Bastion Sage (Mędrzec Bastionu – Elder Guardian of the Sky Sanctuary)

* **Role**: Ancient Scholar & Keeper of the Celestial Portals
* **Appearance**:
  * An elderly scholar draped in flowing indigo robes bordered with silver constellations and Aether crystal brooches.
  * Carries an ancient astrolabe staff that measures the celestial alignment of the castle’s clockwork towers.
* **Function in the Castle**:
  * Welcomes champions at the threshold of the *Aether Sky Sanctuary*.
  * Deciphers forgotten astronomical charts, unlocking higher tiers in the Grimoire of Magic.
  * Explains the historical rupture that destabilized the castle's temporal core and teaches players how to harmonize solar and celestial magic.
* **Key In-Game Dialogue (from `dialogues.ts`)**:
  > **Bastion Sage**: *"Greetings, Guardians. The Sunlit Bastion requires both your blades and serene minds."*

## 5. Master Oakhaven (The Proving Architect) – Historical Ally

* **Role**: Legendary Fencing Master & Mechanical Architect
* **Lore & Legacy**:
  * The visionary weaponmaster who constructed the training grounds, the straw automata, and the counterweight lever locks centuries ago.
  * Authored the *Treatise on the Kinetic Rapier*, which left annotated diagrams scattered across the citadel detailing coyote-time jumps, dash recovery cancelation, and multi-directional angled spellcasting.
  * Manifests as a guiding spiritual echo at rest sanctuaries, evaluating player completion times and scoring achievements.
